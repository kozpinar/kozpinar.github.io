  • Reklamlar Google AdMob ile gösterilir. AdMob, reklam göstermek ve ölçmek için cihaz bilgisi, kaba konum (IP adresinden) ve izin verirseniz reklam kimliği kullanabilir.   • Cihazınızdaki oyun verisi, oyunu sildiğinizde silinir. Reklam kimliğinizi cihaz ayarlarından sıfırlayabilir ya da kısıtlayabilirsiniz.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data the game Rocket Chronicle: V2 to Mars (“the game”) uses and how.

  • There is no game account. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device; they are not sent to a server of ours.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may use device information, approximate location (from the IP address) and, if you allow it, the advertising identifier. In the European Economic Area and the UK your consent is asked for this; on iOS the tracking permission is asked separately. The game works the same if you decline. How Google uses data: policies.google.com.
  • Statistics that do not identify you may be collected to understand how the game is used and why it crashes (Google Firebase). This data is not sold.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
  • Game data on your device is deleted when you delete the game. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date above changes.
